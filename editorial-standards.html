How QuickMark handles educational quality, sourcing, corrections, and public trust maintenance.

Focus

QuickMark

01

Purpose

Topic 1

QuickMark publishes educational product content to help students practise Cambridge-style MCQs with a clear, repeatable workflow.

Public-facing articles and guidance are written to be practical, specific, and revision-focused rather than motivational filler.

02

Authorship and accountability

Topic 2

Content is published under QuickMark Team editorial ownership and tied to active product and study-workflow context.

Accountability for corrections and content quality is centralized through the contact channel so reported issues can be tracked and resolved.

03

Educational value threshold

Topic 3

Content should explain a clear decision, workflow, or exam-practice pattern that helps students act immediately.

Articles that do not provide concrete utility, examples, or structured guidance are revised before publication.

04

Accuracy and review process

Topic 4

Before publication, content is checked for factual consistency with current product behavior and exam-practice context.

During updates, priority is given to correctness over publishing speed, especially on answer interpretation, timing guidance, and workflow recommendations.

05

Sourcing and claims

Topic 5

QuickMark avoids fabricated statistics, unverifiable performance claims, and guaranteed-outcome language.

When operational numbers are referenced publicly, they should be based on internal records or observable product data.

06

Corrections policy

Topic 6

If you report a suspected issue, include the route URL or paper identifier and a short explanation of what appears incorrect.

QuickMark aims to acknowledge actionable correction reports within 3 business days and resolve straightforward content corrections in the next available update cycle.

Submit correction requests through the Contact page.

07

Transparency and independence

Topic 7

QuickMark is an independent educational platform and does not present itself as an official Cambridge product.

Commercial features and policy text are expected to match actual runtime behavior so users can make informed consent and trust decisions.

08

Update cadence and maintenance

Topic 8

Public content is reviewed and refreshed in ongoing release cycles. Update timing varies by issue severity and scope.

Where appropriate, updated timestamps are kept visible so readers can confirm that content is actively maintained.
