01

1. Information We Collect

Clause 1

We collect only the data necessary to operate the platform.

a) Account Information

Users sign in using Google Sign-In. When you do so, we receive and store:

  • your email address
  • your Google profile picture

We do not collect passwords.

b) Usage and Academic Data

To provide marking, progress tracking, and rankings, we store:

  • your answers to MCQs
  • scores and ranks
  • progress history and attempts

c) Technical Information

We do not intentionally store IP addresses. However, request information may be temporarily processed by hosting and authentication providers for security and operational purposes.

02

2. Cookies and Tracking

Clause 2

We use cookies and tracking technologies based on your consent.

a) Essential Cookies

Always active and necessary for the platform to function:

  • browser local storage for login sessions
  • preference storage for theme and settings
  • session cookies for security

b) Analytics Cookies

Only with your consent. We use Google Analytics to understand usage, measure performance, and identify issues.

Google Analytics is configured to anonymize IP addresses and respects your browser's Do Not Track settings.

c) Advertising Cookies

Currently disabled. Advertising cookies and ad serving are switched off while we improve content quality and complete review-readiness updates.

When advertising is re-enabled, it will remain consent-based and this policy section will be updated accordingly.

03

3. How We Use Your Data

Clause 3

Your data is used for platform operation, marking, progress tracking, rankings, stability, and performance improvements.

With analytics consent, aggregated usage data helps improve the platform. Advertising is currently disabled while review-readiness improvements are in progress.

Important: We do not sell or rent your personal academic data to third parties.

04

4. Third-Party Services

Clause 4

QuickMark uses the following third-party service:

  • Firebase (Google) for authentication and backend services

These services process data in accordance with their own privacy policies and industry security standards.
